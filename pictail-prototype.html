Put the details on the picture.

Listings lose the photo the moment a long description starts. Pictail keeps the image in view. Hover on desktop or tap on mobile and the facts pop up right where they belong.

Try the picture See the flow

Today

Buyer opens a house or car listing. The photo is the reason they stopped. Then a wall of text pushes the next photo off-screen. They scroll. They leave. The picture lost.

With Pictail

The photo stays. Details are pinned to the granite, the sunroom, the mileage, the leather, the roof. Cursor or tap reveals a short popup. The picture never leaves the frame.

Hover or tap a pin — details stay on the photo

Seller flow

Upload. Pin. Save. Share. No essay required.

1

Upload the picture

House exterior, kitchen, car quarter-panel, product hero. The photo is the listing.

2

Add details on the photo

Tap a spot. Type a short fact: new HVAC 2024, 42k miles, quartz island, OEM tires. Save it to that point.

3

Buyer hovers or taps

Popup appears on the picture. They never lose the view. Long copy is optional, not the default.

Where it pays off

Real estate

Rooms, upgrades, appliances, lot lines, roof age. Agents stop writing novels. Buyers stay on the photo tour.

Auto sales

Paint, interior wear, options, service records, damage callouts. Trust without a 12-paragraph description.

Anything for sale

Marketplace items, furniture, gear, rentals. One picture carries the facts that used to live below the fold.
